In the dropdown it shows all the songs on that page.
Lets say 2 songs have been completed, then it will show as done above.
If 3, then it will show 3 songs under completed, and then rest under heading your way.
Max 8 songs per page.
Lets say 11 songs have been completed, so on using queue command, the bot will open the second page directly, with 3 songs under completed, and 4 under heading your way, AND the 12th one is under now playing. The dropdown will show all the songs of that page.
If user clicks on any song, the queue goes back to that song or goes forward to that song. Lets say user clicks on 10 song, now the 11th one is also under heading your way, 9th is under completed, 10th is in nowplaying.
